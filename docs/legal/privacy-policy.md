> **DRAFT — requires review by a qualified US attorney before use.** It does not guarantee compliance with the laws of any state or of Washington, DC.

**Who we are.** [Company legal name, address, contact email.]

**Data we process.** Account data (owner and staff names, emails, password hashes, language); business data entered by our customers (their clients' names, phone numbers, emails, residential addresses, property details, encrypted access instructions; helpers' names, contacts, rates, private notes; appointments; financial records); messages (deleted 24 hours after sending); technical data (session records, hashed IP addresses in security events, audit logs).

**Roles.** For data our customers enter about their clients and helpers, our customer is the business responsible for that data and we act as its service provider/processor.

**Use.** To provide, secure, and support the Service; to prevent abuse; to comply with law. We do not sell personal information and do not use customer business data for advertising.

**Sharing.** With infrastructure providers (Cloudflare, Inc., for hosting, database, and storage) under contract, and when required by law. Platform administrators can see only aggregate account metrics; access to private records is not part of normal operations and any privileged access must be justified and audited.

**Security.** Encryption in transit, encrypted access instructions, hashed passwords, role-based access, tenant isolation, audit logs.

**Retention.** See the Messaging and Data Retention Policy.

**Your rights.** Depending on your state (for example California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, and others), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of your data, and to opt out of certain processing. Requests about data a cleaning business holds about you should be sent to that business; we will assist them. Contact: [email].

**Children.** The Service is not directed to children under 13.

**Changes.** We will post updates with a new effective date.
